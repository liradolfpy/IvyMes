# MES MongoDB Collection Inventory


**Document ID:** MES-DATA-DES-001  
**Version:** 1.6
**Status:** CURRENT
**Database platform:** MongoDB (current)  
**Scope:** Logical collection inventory; no sample documents or physical collection schemas are defined here.

## 1. Authority and design basis

The current inventory is derived from the functional baseline in `03_Functional_Structure/FunctionalStructureModules.md` and `FunctionalStructureSections.md`, the project instructions, the requirements conventions in `01_Requirements`, and the current MongoDB decisions in `00_Project_Control/Decisions_Log.md`. All 119 entries remain in scope. The proposed dependency sequence is maintained in `05_Design/Database/Dependancy_Map.md`; this map organizes design order but does not override inventory scope or resolve open decisions. Under GEN-001 / DEC-018, inventory inclusion and descriptive scope require no separate documentation sign-off. Inventory scope does not define absent field-level schemas.

Collection names follow `mes_<moduleCode>_<singularCollectionName>`, using module codes from the functional baseline and lower camelCase singular nouns. The inventory treats each business concept once; multiple functional sections may use the same collection. Field-level specifications must be documented and checked for consistency before use. No frontend, backend, cloud, or deployment technology is implied.

The data-modeling convention requires flat scalar documents, no embedded documents or arrays, and ID-plus-display-value fields for references. Relationship collections below are CURRENT where a many-to-many or repeated association must be represented separately. Their suitability and query implications need review before detailed schemas. No sample documents are included.

## 2. Inventory notation

- **Referenced by** and **References** name logical collections, not physical foreign-key enforcement. `None identified` means no cross-collection relationship is established at inventory level.
- Listing two entities as separate references from one collection does not imply a relationship between those entities. In particular, a Work Center reference and an Asset/Machine reference are independent; no Work Center-to-Machine/Resource association is defined.
- **Dependencies** identify collections or decisions needed to define or use the collection. They do not imply that the dependency's schema is already current.
- **Collection type** uses the project's categories. Where a concept has more than one purpose, its primary type is shown.
- Module ownership indicates the functional home for the collection; cross-module consumers are listed in relationships.

## 13. Cross-module collections and ownership

This inventory avoids duplicating `workOrder`, `shiftHandover`, `downtimeEvent`, `material`, and traceable identity concepts merely because multiple sections use them. Cross-module references do not change the owning module shown in the name. The functional section *Digital Shift Handover* is covered by `mes_prod_shiftHandover`; this ownership is current and should be confirmed.

Plant/site, work center, product/part, unit of measure, routing, and routing-operation collections are included as APPROVED shared foundations because the functional structure explicitly depends on those concepts. Their module ownership is an inventory proposal, not a current ownership decision. A general-purpose user/account and role collection, customer/supplier, and document/evidence payload storage collection are not added because the supplied functional baseline and available requirements do not establish their ownership or scope. `mes_prod_instructionContent` is only a registry of external content locators; it does not store content payloads. Several rows depend on those concepts; their authoritative source must be decided or located before detailed schemas. Existing MES concepts should be reused once their owning module and authoritative collection are current.

## 16. Decisions and issues to resolve before collection schemas

1. **Collection-specific status semantics:** Each collection defines its own status vocabulary; DEC-011 records Production Execution allowed values and meanings; the corresponding field-level specifications are not present in this workspace. No common status vocabulary is imposed.


2. **Flat-document modeling:** The flat-document rule and separate-record approach are established by `DATA-001` and `DEC-003`. Collection designs must document query patterns, indexes, and consistency rules for repeated records; no collection-specific nested-document or array exception is permitted.
3. **Shared master ownership:** `mes_prod_operation` is the current reusable operation master for Production Execution under `DEC-003`. Confirm authoritative ownership/source and scope for current product, plant/site, work center/resource, routing, and UOM collections; decide whether user/role, customer/supplier, and document/evidence references are MES-owned or externally mastered.
4. **Work order distinction:** Approved `DEC-004` defines `productionOrder` as macro-level authorization/batch progress and `workOrder` as the granular MES execution task. Confirm decomposition cardinality, child quantity allocation/reconciliation, and whether each production order must have at least one work order.
5. **Andon case/event model:** APPROVED `DEC-005` separates one current-state Andon case from its individual transition events; current `DEC-012` defines event types and meanings. Confirm allowed status transitions, reopen behavior, event correction/retention policy, and whether an escalation event may have multiple recipients.
6. **Identity strategy:** The 16-digit string ID convention exists, but the generator is open. Production Execution business-number patterns and scopes are defined under DEC-013; scopes for other modules and sequence allocation mechanisms remain open in `DocumentIdAndReferenceConvention.md`.
7. **Reference snapshots:** Confirm whether denormalized names in transaction documents remain historical snapshots or are synchronized after master changes.
8. **Lifecycle/deletion:** Confirm retirement and physical-deletion policy and the lifecycle vocabularies for master/configuration records.
9. **Cross-domain posting:** Define how material consumption/movement/inventory balances avoid duplicate posting, and how production downtime aligns with machine-state events.
10. **Requirement traceability:** No collection-specific functional requirements were available in the current project files. Obtain or document the detailed requirements before treating this proposal as a complete requirements-derived inventory.
11. **Work-instruction content registry:** Approved `DEC-009` establishes `mes_prod_instructionContent` as a flat registry of exact-version external locators referenced by Work Instructions. Provider-specific locator resolution remains an integration concern; the registry does not store the content payload.
12. **Specification availability and status:** Each listed collection has `CURRENT — inventory scope only` status in this inventory under GEN-001 as of 2026-10-11. This does not mean a field-level collection specification exists or is current; each field-level design must be present, current, and checked for consistency; no separate sign-off is required under GEN-001.
